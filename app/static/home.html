How it works, step by step

What happens at each step, what CareerPilot does, and what you decide.

  1. 1

    Discover jobs

    Live now

    CareerPilot checks public job boards and company sources every 6 hours, or whenever you press Fetch new jobs. Each job is cleaned into the same format with its title, company, location, description, date, and original link.

    You: add your keywords and browse the results.

  2. 2

    Remove the noise

    Live now

    The same job from two sources is shown once. Jobs that disappear from their source are marked closed. Keywords, location, date, and seniority filters narrow the list instantly.

    You: open a job and read the full details.

  3. 3

    Build your profile

    Live now

    You upload your CV. Education, skills, experience, and projects are extracted, and every fact is linked to the exact line it came from.

    You: confirm each fact and set your preferences.

  4. 4

    Match and rank

    Live now

    Each job's required skills are compared with your verified profile. You get a score with its reasons: which skills match, which are missing, and the evidence for each one.

    You: choose which jobs are worth applying to.

  5. 5

    Tailor your CV

    Live now

    Your most relevant projects and skills are moved forward for the chosen job. Every line is checked against your confirmed facts, and skills you cannot show are reported instead of added.

    You: read what changed, then download the PDF.

  6. 6

    Prepare the application

    Live now

    A cover letter and a checklist are created with your tailored CV, then saved together in one folder for each job. Anything your confirmed facts cannot support is flagged.

    You: edit the letter and clear the checklist.

  7. 7

    Review and submit

    Live now

    Everything is shown together for a final check. Nothing goes out without your approval, and the official application page opens for you to submit.

    You: approve and submit it yourself.

  8. 8

    Track the outcome

    Live now

    Each application moves from Saved to Applied, Interview, and finally Rejected or Offer. Dates, links, and follow-up reminders stay in one place.

    You: update the status when you hear back.
